## E. Prohibited claims before validation

Do not claim:

- complete traceability;
- executable specification;
- parity;
- immutable history;
- accountable closure;
- decision effectiveness;
- field damage diagnosis;
- predictive maintenance;
- universal novelty.

Use “candidate contribution”, “design requirement” or “prepared for validation” until evidence exists.
