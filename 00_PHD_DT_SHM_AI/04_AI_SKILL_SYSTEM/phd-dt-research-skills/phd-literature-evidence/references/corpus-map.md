## Reading order for a prior-art question

1. `01_CURRENT_RESEARCH_CONTEXT_20260804.md` — is this already classified?
2. The relevant Round report — what does the deep read actually say?
3. `00_MASTER_INDEX_CURRENT_20260804.md` — is there newer evidence?
4. Only then, the wider Zotero or discovery material.

If steps 1–3 answer the question, stop there and cite them. Going wider costs context and risks introducing unverified material into a settled decision.
