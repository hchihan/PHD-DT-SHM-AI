I've compiled the methodological literature review on the report. Here's a brief summary of what it covers:

**Highest-value findings** for your locked chain (Sensor_ID → BIM_GUID → Condition_Evidence_ID → Inspection_Record_ID → Action_Status):

1. **Lorvão Antunes et al. (2024)** — Ontology-based BIM-AMS integration in European Highways: this is the closest structural analogue. It defines 13 competency questions *before* implementation via the NeOn methodology's ORSD, validates via CQ-answering plus OOPS! pitfall detection, and explicitly chains SSN sensors → ifcOWL/PhysicalObject GUID → Risk-and-Condition Observations → Inspections.[^1]
2. **Peralta et al., BrIO (2026)** — Ontology-based representation of bridge inspections: 10 pre-registered CQs converted to SPARQL tests, validated on a real bridge case (Nibelungen Bridge) with IFC-to-RDF conversion establishing the BIM_GUID anchor and explicit inspection-to-inspection temporal chaining (`dicp:precedes`) — essentially a template for Inspection_Record_ID sequencing.[^2]
3. **Keet \& Khan (2024/2025)**  provide the formal theory of CQ types (Scoping vs. Validation CQs) with the key rule that Validation CQs must be answerable within the ontology's logic — directly reusable to formalize each chain link as a pre-registered, testable claim.[^3][^4]
4. **SHACL-based schema validation papers**  supply reproducible constraint-testing patterns (one rule = one shape = one automated test) transferable to enforcing referential integrity between BIM_GUID and evidence/inspection records.[^5][^6][^7]
