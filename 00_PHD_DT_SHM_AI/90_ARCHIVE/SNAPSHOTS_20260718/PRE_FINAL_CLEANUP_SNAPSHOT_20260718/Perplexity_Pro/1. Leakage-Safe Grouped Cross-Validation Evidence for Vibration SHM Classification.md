## Reasons for inclusion and limitations

Goodarzi et al. 2025  and Matania et al. 2024  are included as the two strongest methodological anchors because both explicitly quantify the accuracy inflation caused by leakage-prone splitting on accelerometer/vibration condition-monitoring data, directly parallel to the thesis's LORO justification; their limitation is that neither uses a steel-frame bolted-connection rig. Zhang, Biswal & Wang 2020  and Ramasso et al. 2024  are included as direct structural precedents (steel-frame bolted connection; bolted-joint campaign structure) but neither implements a fully formalized grouped cross-validation protocol comparable to LORO — this is the boundary the Living Evidence Base already records as "not leakage-safe protocol proof," and this report does not extend that boundary. Eraliev, Lee & Lee 2022  is included only as a strong-adjacent caveat source. No source in this set claims a calibrated FEM link, stiffness-degradation inference, or lab-to-field classifier transfer; none is used here to support such claims, consistent with the locked scope.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

## Explicit gap boundary

No reviewed source combines all three elements of the thesis's WP1 design — (1) a scaled steel-frame connection rig, (2) accelerometer-only sensing, and (3) a formal leave-one-run-out/leave-one-group-out protocol with quantified leakage-versus-no-leakage comparison — in one study. This is stated only as an observation across the sources retrieved in this search, not as a certified literature gap, per the Space's rule against claiming gaps from search summaries alone.
