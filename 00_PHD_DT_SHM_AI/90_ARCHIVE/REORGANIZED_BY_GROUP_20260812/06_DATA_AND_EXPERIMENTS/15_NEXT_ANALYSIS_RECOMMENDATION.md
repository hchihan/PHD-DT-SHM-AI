## Immediate priority (before any further modelling)

1. **Resolve the sensor column-position instability found in Stage A** (see `00_EXECUTIVE_REPORT.md` §1 and `03_DATA_QUALITY_AND_PROVENANCE_LOG.csv`, check `sensor_column_position_stability`). Campaign 1 and Campaign 2 CSV column blocks do **not** consistently correspond to the same physical sensor across all 72 files each (confirmed independently via static-gravity fingerprint clustering; consistent with the prior project's own audit). Campaign 3 is clean. Before any single-sensor (S1/S2/S3) result from Campaign 1 or Campaign 2 is used in a Paper 1 claim, each of those 144 files needs a per-file, fingerprint-based physical-sensor reassignment (not a column-position assumption), and every within-campaign/cross-campaign result that used single- or paired-sensor configs from Campaign 1/2 needs to be re-run against the corrected assignment. This is the single most important open item.
2. **Complete the CNN seed sweep.** Only `seed=42` was run. Seeds `123` and `2026` (task minimum of 3) are outstanding for both within-campaign LORO and cross-campaign LOCO. Compute cost measured in this session: ~35–45 s per CNN fit (TensorFlow/Keras, CPU-only, 2 vCPU sandbox) including session-startup overhead: full remaining matrix ≈ 250+ fits, i.e. several hours of wall-clock time under this sandbox's 45-second-per-command ceiling. Recommend running on a machine without that per-command ceiling.
3. **Complete the within-campaign LORO CNN matrix.** Only Campaign 1 / S1_Ax / 4 of 6 folds were run for CNN; Campaign 1's other configs and all of Campaign 2/3's five configs × six folds are outstanding. Feature-baseline (RF/SVM/LogReg) coverage is complete for this matrix; CNN is not.
4. **Explain the early-stopping-at-epoch-11 pattern.** Several CNN folds (notably Campaign 1 / S1_Ax) stopped after 11 epochs with macro-F1 ≈ 0.10 (nearly single-class collapse). A tiny-set overfit check (this session) confirms the architecture/training loop itself can reach ~97–99% train accuracy given enough epochs (no `patience`), so the pipeline is not fundamentally broken — but `patience=10` combined with a small, possibly noisy validation split may be truncating training before the model escapes an early local optimum on the harder single-axis configs. Worth an explicit ablation (same seed, patience=20) as a documented sensitivity check, not a silent architecture change.
5. **Extend confusion matrices / per-window predictions-with-provenance to the full fold matrix.** Only three representative folds (one within-Campaign1, one within-Campaign2, one cross-campaign) have saved confusion matrices and per-window predictions in this pass; the other ~70 folds only have aggregate fold-level metrics.
6. **Training curves (13_TRAINING_CURVES/) were not retained** during the CNN sweep (only final epoch count was logged, not per-epoch loss/accuracy history). If curve plots are required for Paper 1, re-run the specific folds needed with history capture enabled.
7. **Investigate the Campaign 3 within-campaign variability.** Campaign 3 RF within-campaign macro-F1 ranges from 0.218 to 0.954 across folds/configs (std 0.184) — much wider than Campaign 2 (std 0.091). Worth a dedicated per-run/per-state breakdown before treating any single Campaign 3 fold as representative.
8. **Hyperparameter tuning is explicitly out of scope for this pass**, per the task brief (Section 14): only once G1 sanity checks, permutation control, run-level reproducibility and cross-campaign evaluation are all in an accepted state should Leave-One-Campaign-Out (outer) / Group-CV-by-Run-ID-within-training-campaigns (inner) tuning begin.
