## Part 1 -- Gap-by-gap status (corrected 8-gap taxonomy)

### GAP-A -- Verified component identity against actual BIM/IFC source
**Status: ESTABLISHED.** Multiple corpus papers verify a component identifier against a real BIM/IFC source, not merely assume the linkage: MULTI_01 (Lee, Eastman & Solihin) validates GUID uniqueness/format/round-trip correctness against real CV2.0/COBie exchange data; G2_02 (Stolk & McGlinn) and G2_03 (Oraskari et al.) both use `IfcGloballyUniqueId`/`ifc:globalId_IfcRoot` as SHACL `focusNode` identifiers validated against real (R2RML-converted geospatial, and BOT Duplex Apartment) ifcOWL graphs; MULTI_02 (Lorvão Antunes et al.) and MULTI_03 (Kim et al.) both extract and use real IFC-derived identifiers (GUID and internal STEP entity IDs respectively) in real pilot/case-study deployments; **G1_02 Borkowski et al. (2026, full text now confirmed)** extracts real `hasIFCGuid` values from 3 independently exported Revit/IFC files in its case study. **Paper 2 CANNOT claim novelty for "verifying/extracting a component identifier from an actual BIM/IFC source"** — this is established territory across at least six corpus papers using at least three distinct techniques (rule-based validation, SHACL, ontology-instance extraction).

### GAP-B -- Identity persistence / continuity across model revisions
**Status: PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED.** The general framing and a concrete, working (if small-scale and manual) solution now exist, full-text-confirmed: **G1_02 (Borkowski et al. 2026)** proposes a DOLCE-grounded ontology in which a new, GUID-independent, persistent URI identity (`BuildingElement` as a DOLCE endurant) is minted and shown, via a real 3-file Revit/IFC case study, to preserve identity across successive life-cycle-phase files, validated by the HermiT reasoner and 8/8 correctly-answered competency questions. The paper explicitly claims priority: "to our knowledge, the first construction ontology that grounds element identity across lifecycle phases in a foundational ontology." Supporting/contrasting evidence: MULTI_02 (Lorvão Antunes) self-acknowledges this problem as unaddressed ("manual mapping between ontology and ifcOWL instances" is a stated limitation); MULTI_03 (Kim et al.) explicitly notes BIM models are "seldom updated" in FM practice, sidestepping rather than solving the problem; MULTI_01 (Lee, Eastman & Solihin) validates GUID round-trip correctness only within a single export/import cycle, not across independently authored successive-phase models; G1_01 (Fang et al.) tests GUID compliance across a single conversion step only. **Net assessment, now full-text-confirmed rather than abstract-inferred:** the *general* claim "IFC GUID identity does not persist across life-cycle-phase files, and this can be solved with a persistent identifier layered on top" is **no longer available to Paper 2 as a novel claim** — Borkowski both names and solves it, with a real (if small-scale, manual) implementation. What remains open, confirmed absent from Borkowski and every other corpus paper: (i) an **automated or deterministically validated** identity-linking step (Borkowski's own matching step is explicitly manual/expert-judgement); (ii) identity persistence **integrated** with a multi-record chain (Sensor_ID → Condition_Evidence_ID → Inspection_Record_ID) rather than tracking one endurant's own property history in isolation.

### GAP-C -- Condition evidence provenance
**Status: PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED.** Component techniques for provenance exist in isolated papers but none combine full condition-evidence provenance (source + measurement + processing + human-actor) anchored to a persistent BIM component identity. MULTI_02 (Lorvão Antunes) models an `Agent` taking a "Sensor" role for Observations (source, PARTIAL) with a numeric 1-5 indicator plus descriptive result (measurement, PARTIAL) but no processing or human-actor provenance. G3_01 (Ciccarese et al., PAV ontology) provides a mature, general-purpose vocabulary distinguishing source (`pav:providedBy`/`retrievedFrom`/`sourceAccessedAt`), processing (`pav:importedFrom` = transcription/transformation), and human-actor (`pav:authoredBy`/`curatedBy`/`createdBy`) provenance — but this is a document/resource-centric vocabulary from the biomedical-semantics domain, never applied to BIM inspection records. G2_01 (Sacks et al., SeeBridge) implies a detection pipeline (source, PARTIAL) with defect imagery mapped to model surfaces (a form of evidence-source linkage) but no explicit provenance schema. **G1_02 Borkowski (full text)** provides only a bare source-file backlink (`hasIFCGuid`, "backtracking to the source file") — confirmed as the narrowest form of provenance in the corpus, with no measurement, processing, or human-actor dimension at all. **No corpus paper combines all four provenance types anchored to a persistent, GUID-independent BIM component identity.**

### GAP-D -- Evidence validity / applicability scope
**Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED_IN_TARGETED_CORPUS.** This verdict is now returned with full-text confidence (all 12 corpus papers, including Borkowski, have been read in full — see the `evidence_validity_scope` column of [05_TARGETED_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.csv](05_TARGETED_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.csv)), per this task's explicit rule that this verdict may only be used "khi đã kiểm tra đủ full-text evidence," not from a quick scan. Using the strict definition (valid_from/valid_to, operating condition, protocol/configuration/confidence context, and explicit supersession/"do not reuse outside this context" rules for a *piece of evidence* — as distinguished from case-study scope, asset type, sample size, project scope, geographical scope, ontology scope, and from `model_version_context`), the closest partial analogs found were:
- **MULTI_02 (Lorvão Antunes):** `prov:atTime` timestamps used with `MAX` aggregation to select the "most recent" inspection result (CQ10) — an implicit, recency-based supersession mechanism, but no explicit valid-time bound or applicability-condition rule. PARTIAL.
- **MULTI_03 (Kim et al.):** a bare `Date` field on maintenance-work records, with no applicability or supersession rule. PARTIAL.
- **G3_01 (Ciccarese et al., PAV):** `pav:previousVersion`/`pav:derivedFrom` provide a genuine, general supersession mechanism (directly analogous to "superseded_by"), plus per-role timestamps — the strongest partial precedent in the corpus — but PAV has no valid_from/valid_to bound and no operating-condition/protocol/configuration-applicability concept; it captures *when* a version was superseded, not *under what conditions* a piece of content remains valid to (re)use. PARTIAL.
- **G1_02 (Borkowski, full text confirmed):** explicitly and completely absent. The paper's `precedes`-ordered, disjoint `DesignPhase`/`AsBuiltPhase`/`FacilityManagementPhase` subclasses are a `model_version_context` mechanism (which phase/file a value belongs to), not an evidence-validity rule — this distinction is confirmed directly from the full text and is exactly the conflation this correction pass warns against.
- All remaining 8 papers: NOT_REPORTED, confirmed via full-text reading.

**No corpus paper — including the newly full-text-verified Borkowski — implements an explicit evidence-validity/applicability rule in the strict sense defined for this correction pass.** This is a genuine, full-text-confirmed open gap, distinct from (and not to be conflated with) `model_version_context`, which several papers (G1_02, G2_03) do address.

### GAP-E -- Inspection-record accountability (performer/reviewer/approver/status/supersession)
**Status: NOT_ESTABLISHED_IN_TARGETED_CORPUS -- confirmed open gap, and the strongest surviving one.** Across every G3-relevant corpus paper with real or near-real inspection/work-record content (MULTI_02, MULTI_03, G2_01 SeeBridge, G3_02 Wan et al.), none formalizes a reviewer/approver/status/superseded-record schema as an explicit, queryable data-model field. **G3_02 (Wan et al., the most complete real-deployment case, a 2019 real cable-stayed bridge BMS)** remains the clearest example: it *narratively* describes a manager-review step for both inspection submissions and repair completions, but this is never captured as a named attribute in its own formal IFC/IFD attribute-set tables. **G1_02 Borkowski (full text confirmed)** independently reconfirms this gap from a completely different angle: its crack/inspection/repair content (a real damage-inspection scenario embedded in the case study — "following a technical inspection, the column is reinforced with CFRP") is folded into bare property-value changes on the endurant, with **zero** accountability fields anywhere in its 9-class schema or its event-layer extension (`PhaseTransitionEvent` records only source-phase/target-phase/affected-element, never who/what performed the transition). **This is now the strongest, most independently corroborated gap in the entire targeted review** — confirmed absent in both a real deployed engineering-practice system (G3_02) and a rigorously formalized 2026 ontology paper (G1_02) that otherwise solves a closely adjacent problem.

### GAP-F -- Forward + reverse evidence↔component↔inspection traceability
**Status: PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED.** Component-level (single-hop) traceability is demonstrated in several papers. MULTI_03 (Kim et al.) achieves the strongest result — SPARQL retrieval of related work records by IFC component, plus reverse retrieval of shared-element records across adjacent spaces (both YES). MULTI_02 (Lorvão Antunes) demonstrates forward/reverse traceability via SPARQL competency questions (both PARTIAL). G2_01 (Sacks) demonstrates forward traceability (rule result → source text → failing BIM object) but not reverse (NOT_REPORTED). **G1_02 Borkowski (full text confirmed):** its 8 competency questions (CQ1–CQ8) support forward/reverse querying, but strictly *within one endurant's own phase/property history* — never across a Sensor→Evidence→Inspection-record chain spanning multiple distinct identifier types (both PARTIAL, confirmed via full text, not merely inferred). **No corpus paper demonstrates forward+reverse traceability that crosses three or more distinct record-type boundaries (sensor reading → condition evidence → inspection record) with formal, queryable cross-references** — this multi-hop combination remains open.

### GAP-G -- Deterministic expected-answer / pass-fail validation
**Status: ESTABLISHED** as a general technique, across multiple independent methodological families. **G1_02 Borkowski (full text confirmed)** provides a particularly clean example: 8 competency questions, each formally mapped in a published table to the exact classes/relations/axioms required, "all eight questions answered correctly" — genuine deterministic expected-answer validation. Also established via: MULTI_02 (competency questions), G2_04 (quantified SHACL pass/fail, 372→14 violations across 2 cycles on a real 369,054-triple building), G2_01 (a 3-class conformance-error taxonomy: syntactic/semantic-structure/omission), G2_05 (manual-ground-truth comparison matching automated results against real BREEAM assessment on a real London office building). **Paper 2 CANNOT claim general novelty for "using deterministic/expected-answer pass-fail validation methodology"** — this is thoroughly precedented via at least four distinct techniques (competency questions, quantified constraint-violation counts, conformance-error taxonomies, ground-truth comparison). The novelty, if any, must lie in *applying* this established methodology to the specific multi-hop chain Paper 2 targets, not in the validation paradigm itself.

### GAP-H -- Deliberate invalid-link testing (orphan/duplicate/stale/wrong model version/mismatched component/conflicting binding)
**Status: PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED.** Individual negative-test techniques are established but confined to single-model or single-conversion-pair scope. **G2_03 (Oraskari et al.)** is the strongest precedent: concrete, working, GitHub-published `FILTER NOT EXISTS` SHACL-SPARQL patterns detecting missing GUID counterparts and missing relationships, plus MD5-checksum-based drift detection for named element instances — tested against two independent real IFC-to-LBD converters of the same underlying model. **G2_02 (Stolk & McGlinn)** demonstrates concrete detection of malformed enumeration values and incorrect class-typing. **MULTI_01** demonstrates GUID uniqueness/duplicate negative rules within a single CV2.0/COBie exchange cycle. **G1_02 Borkowski (full text confirmed — this is a key, newly clarified finding):** performs exactly **one** deliberate negative/invalid test, and it is at the **ontology-schema/axiom level** (intentionally assigning an individual dual `BuildingElement`/`LifecyclePhase` classification to test the disjointness axiom, correctly flagged inconsistent by the HermiT reasoner) — **not** at the identity-mapping/data-instance level. None of the specific Q5/GAP-H taxonomy (orphan, duplicate, stale, wrong-model-version, mismatched-component, conflicting-binding GUID) was tested anywhere in Borkowski's paper, despite GUID regeneration being its own central motivating problem. **In every corpus paper, this negative testing is performed within a single model or between two representations of the identical underlying model (a format-conversion pair), never across independently authored, temporally separated life-cycle-phase model revisions, and never across a genuinely cross-system chain spanning a sensor reading, a condition-evidence record, and a human-authored inspection record.** This specific multi-hop, cross-system combination remains a confirmed, genuine open gap.

## Part 2 -- Synthesis / Final Decision

**1. Sau full-text Borkowski, GAP-B còn đứng ở mức nào?**

GAP-B is now **PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED with full-text confirmation**, not merely abstract-inferred as in the prior pass. The *general* claim — "IFC GUID identity does not persist across life-cycle-phase files, and a persistent identifier layered on top of GUIDs can solve this" — is now **confirmed, not just plausible**, to be already published, implemented (at small scale), and reasoner-validated by Borkowski et al. (2026), who additionally claim explicit priority over this exact territory. What survives as open, also full-text-confirmed: (a) an *automated or deterministically validated* identity-linking mechanism (Borkowski's is explicitly manual); (b) identity persistence *integrated* into a multi-record, cross-system chain rather than tracked for one element's own property history in isolation.

**2. Borkowski có thực sự đe dọa Paper 2 core contribution hay chỉ đe dọa một sub-claim về identity persistence?**

**Only a sub-claim — confirmed, not merely asserted, via full-text reading.** Borkowski's paper is now confirmed, systematically, section by section, to be **silent** on 6 of Paper 2's 8 stated core-contribution components: evidence provenance (only a bare source-file backlink, no measurement/processing/human-actor dimension — GAP-C), evidence validity/applicability (confirmed completely absent — GAP-D), accountable inspection records (zero accountability fields anywhere — GAP-E), cross-system forward/reverse traceability (confined to one endurant's own phase history — GAP-F), and deliberate identity-*mapping* negative/invalid-case testing (only one schema-axiom test, not an identity-integrity test — GAP-H). It also does not achieve automated/deterministic identity-linking (GAP-B, narrower framing). It threatens **only** the identity-governance component (GAP-A/B) of Paper 2's core contribution, and even there, only the *conceptual/small-scale-demonstrated* framing, not an automated or chain-integrated one. **Paper 2's core contribution as an integrated 8-component whole is not threatened by Borkowski.**

**3. GAP-D validity/applicability scope: established? partial? open? insufficient evidence?**

**OPEN — NOT_ESTABLISHED_IN_TARGETED_CORPUS, with full-text confidence (not EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT).** All 12 corpus papers have now been read in full (including Borkowski). The strict definition (valid-time bounds, operating-condition/protocol/configuration applicability, explicit supersession/"do not reuse outside this context" rules *for a piece of evidence*) was checked systematically against every paper. The closest partial precedents (PAV's `previousVersion`/`derivedFrom` supersession mechanism; Lorvão Antunes' `prov:atTime`-based recency selection; Kim et al.'s bare timestamp) all fall short of the full definition — none combine a valid-time bound with an explicit applicability/reuse condition. This is a genuine, full-text-confirmed, defensible open gap for Paper 2.

**4. 2-4 Paper 2 contribution claims nào còn đứng vững?**

(a) A verifiable traceability **framework** integrating GUID-anchored component identity with condition-evidence and inspection records into a single, explicit, queryable, cross-system chain (Sensor_ID → BIM_GUID/IFC identity → Condition_Evidence_ID → Inspection_Record_ID) — no corpus paper, including Borkowski, combines all three record types with formal cross-references keyed on a persistent identity.

(b) An **automated or deterministically auditable identity-linking mechanism** across model revisions/phases — explicitly distinguishing Paper 2 from Borkowski's confirmed-manual identity-matching step, while reusing Borkowski's endurant/perdurant conceptual framing where useful as prior art rather than reinventing it.

(c) **Formalized, first-class inspection-record accountability** (performer/reviewer/approver/status/supersession) as explicit, queryable schema fields — addressing the practice-vs-data-model gap confirmed concretely in both G3_02 (a real deployed 2019 bridge BMS) and now G1_02 (a rigorous 2026 ontology paper that otherwise solves an adjacent problem but has zero accountability fields) — optionally informed by, but domain-adapted from, PAV/PROV-O (G3_01) rather than inventing a new role taxonomy from scratch.

(d) An **explicit evidence-validity/applicability rule** (valid-time, protocol/configuration context, supersession-of-evidence) integrated with (a)-(c), plus a **deliberate, multi-hop, cross-system negative/invalid-link testing protocol** extending the single-model/single-conversion-pair techniques demonstrated piecemeal in G2_02/G2_03/G2_04/MULTI_01/G1_02 to the full chain in (a) — both confirmed as genuinely open (GAP-D, GAP-H) even after Borkowski's full-text reading.

**5. Có cần đổi RQ2 không?**

**MINOR_REFINEMENT.** The core research question stands. The now-full-text-confirmed Borkowski finding sharpens rather than undermines it: the novel element is not "identity persistence across model revisions" in isolation (now confirmed claimed, at least conceptually/small-scale, by Borkowski) but the **integration** of identity governance with evidence provenance, evidence validity, accountable records, cross-system traceability, deterministic validation, and negative testing into one coherent, verifiable chain. Recommend RQ2's phrasing continue to foreground "verifiable" and "accountable," and explicitly distinguish "persistent identity" (now partially precedented) from "automated/deterministic, chain-integrated identity governance" (still open).

**6. Có cần đổi Gate 2 không?**

**MINOR_REFINEMENT.** Gate 2 (BIM information readiness/governance) evidence is now stronger and more precise: Borkowski (full text) is confirmed as the closest identity-governance-specific novelty threat, with explicit, citable, confirmed boundaries (manual matching; no accountability; no evidence-validity concept). No structural change to Gate 2's criteria is indicated — the correction strengthens the evidentiary basis for satisfying it, and clarifies that Gate 2 must explicitly address model/revision governance (where G1_02 and G2_03 offer citable prior art) as distinct from evidence-validity governance (which remains genuinely open, per GAP-D).

**7. Có cần đổi Gate 3 không?**

**MINOR_REFINEMENT.** Gate 3 (BIM binding + evidence integration + deterministic traceability validation) gains a concrete, full-text-confirmed methodological precedent in Borkowski's competency-question validation design (8 CQs, each formally mapped to required ontology constructs, "answered correctly" as a deterministic pass criterion) — directly reusable as a validation-design template — while also gaining a clear, confirmed illustration of what Gate 3 must do that Borkowski does not: automate the identity-linking step, and test identity-*mapping* integrity (not just schema-axiom consistency) with deliberate negative cases across a multi-hop chain.
